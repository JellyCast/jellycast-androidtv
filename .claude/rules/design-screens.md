# Design: layout per screen

Continuation of `design.md`. How you move through these screens with the remote: `design-navigation.md`. When in doubt about effects or image sizes: `design.md`. Quoted UI texts are the English strings; Paper artboard names stay as they are in Paper (Dutch).

## Screen layouts (chosen 2026-10-08)

Goal: as few clicks as possible to playback. Movies and series are separate choices, because before watching you already know which of the two it will be.

- **Home** = a bar at the top with **Home**, **Series**, **Movies** and **Library** (plus Settings as a small icon; chosen 2026-10-10, Surprise me became the Home section), and below it the content of the chosen section. The app always opens on the Home section (chosen 2026-10-10; previously the last used section). Back from a detail screen or the player lands on the section you were in. Back on the section bar goes to the Home tab; there pressing Back twice closes the app (chosen 2026-10-12).
- **Groups, the same for Series and Movies** (chosen 2026-10-09). With ↓ you go through a fixed order: **1. Continue watching · 2. New** (most recently added first, only not yet watched; for Movies all unwatched movies with no time limit or maximum, for Series only from the last 30 days and max. 20; chosen 2026-10-10) **· 3+. one group per genre**, alphabetical (A–Z within a genre). Only genres with ≥ 3 titles get a group. Genre names come in English from the server and are translated to the UI language via the `genre_*` strings in strings.xml (mapping in `core/common/GenreResources.kt`; e.g. Action → Actie, War & Politics → Oorlog & politiek in Dutch; unknown = as the server gives it). "Alphabetical" is in that language (Collator), and redone at the next load after a language change. "All series" and "All movies" are gone (chosen 2026-10-10): everything A–Z, search and sort is in the **Library**. An empty group (e.g. nothing to continue) is skipped.
- **Series**: the same groups as rows stacked vertically (hero at the top). "Continue watching" = one card per series ("S3 · E5", with progress bar), only for started series, **by time** (chosen 2026-10-10, like Netflix and Apple TV): the most recently touched episode counts, even if another has a higher number. Half watched = resume that one; fully watched = the first unwatched one after it. No threshold: any progress counts. Episode 1 at position 0 (series only briefly opened) doesn't count as started (chosen 2026-10-09). The row order is also by time, most recently touched first. The Continue button on the series page and the suggestion on Home use the same rule; "New" = series with new episodes (not if you've watched the whole series); directly below, only for Series, **"Not started yet"**: all series of which you haven't watched or half watched any episode, A–Z (chosen 2026-10-10); the genres are poster rows. **OK on a landscape episode card (Continue watching) plays that episode directly; holding OK opens the options panel (as in Movies): Mark as watched, Mark as unwatched (only in progress), Go to series (series page with focus on that episode; chosen 2026-10-15), with the "[OK] hold for more" hint next to the row heading; OK on a series poster opens the series page** with focus on "Continue" (chosen 2026-10-09; until phase 4 a poster played the next episode). Back always lands on the card you came from.
- **Movies: two views, chosen in Settings** ("Movie view", chosen 2026-10-09; default **detail**). Both use the same shelves and the same navigation (↑/↓ = shelf, ←/→ = movie, entering a shelf = movie 1).
  - **Detail view** (Paper: "Films (detailweergave)"): one movie per screen in the structure of the movie detail (backdrop, logo, metadata, 3 lines, tech line), with the top bar and shelf indicator above it. One button **Watch**/**Continue watching** (focus); next to it the hint "[OK] hold for more". A short OK (on release) plays; holding OK opens a panel on the right (like the player menus): From the start (only with progress), Mark as watched, Mark as unwatched (when watched, and for a movie in progress to clear its progress; chosen 2026-10-15), Full description. Back closes the panel. Marking as watched removes the movie from New and Continue watching immediately; you then see the movie that slides into its place, or the previous one if it was the last (chosen 2026-10-10). Bottom left "‹ previous · n / m · next ›". No separate focus on the button needed for ←/→, so shelf n = n× ↓. While browsing (holding a key): flat dark spine color instead of the backdrop, no description and tech line (the space stays); the backdrop comes after ~300 ms of rest. Back: straight to the section bar (the shelf silently returns to movie 1 of shelf 1).
  - **Bookcase**: below.
- **Movies = a DVD bookcase** (chosen 2026-10-08). Series is businesslike ("where was I"), movies you pick by feel; hence a different overview.
  - Top bar (76 dp): sections, followed by the **shelf indicator** (not focusable): shows the current shelf in bold with the previous and next beside it ("… New · **Action** · Adventure · Drama …"), because with genres there are too many to show them all.
  - **Switch shelves with ↑/↓**, in that fixed order: shelf n = n× ↓ from the top bar. ↑ from the first shelf goes to the sections. Entering a shelf = item 1 (see `design-navigation.md`).
  - Shelves: Continue watching, New, then one shelf per genre (see "Groups"). There is no separate Categories shelf.
  - Empty space at the end of a short shelf shows "Next shelf ↓ <name>".
  - If there are movies off-screen on the left or right, a black 120 dp fade at that screen edge with a small arrow (‹ or ›) in it (chosen 2026-10-09): a calmer picture and a hint that ←/→ goes further. No overflow on a side = no fade.
  - Below it, from top to bottom, one row of **vertical spines** (44 × 431 dp, 4 dp between them; 6 dp below the top bar and the 27 dp safe area at the bottom kept free, chosen 2026-10-09: to the edge felt too full), built like a DVD case:
    - **top**: the poster, cropped to 44 × 78 dp (`fillWidth=88`), with a 1 dp seam below it;
    - **middle**: the logo, rotated 90° (reads bottom to top), max. 30 × 212 dp. No logo: the title rotated as text;
    - **bottom**: resolution (4K/HD, `labelMedium` heavy), below it the HDR type (DV/HDR) or else ATMOS, a dash, and the age rating in a circle (12/16; "R" etc. for non-Dutch ratings; nothing if missing). Studio logos don't exist (the server has no studio images), so we don't show them;
    - **case edge**: 1 dp dark on the left, 1 dp light sheen on the right. Plain rectangles, no gradient;
    - text and lines on a light spine (see "Spine color") are dark.
  - All data comes from the item itself (`OfficialRating`, `MediaStreams`: width, `VideoRangeType`, audio profile), so no extra requests per spine.
  - The focused movie is **open**, a 500 dp wide panel: backdrop, gradient to the spine color, logo, metadata and two buttons stacked: **Watch** (or **Continue watching** with the time left, if the movie is in progress) and **Details** (chosen 2026-10-09; the description is on the movie detail, and usually you want to watch right away). The open panel is itself the focus; no extra line or border (amber line removed 2026-10-09, added nothing). On item 1 the panel starts at the left margin; further right the row shifts so the panel stays in a fixed place, with a few closed spines on the left (the first cut in half).
  - → / ←: the panel flips directly to the next/previous movie (no width animation; at most a 150 ms translation via `graphicsLayer`). During key repeat the panel shows only spine color + logo; the backdrop comes after ~300 ms of rest.
  - Opening a movie = Watch selected. **↓ on Watch selects Details, ↓ on Details goes to the next shelf**; ↑ on Details back to Watch. The buttons have no focus of their own (it stays on the movie), so ←/→ stays one press per movie. Note: therefore shelf n from Watch is no longer n× ↓ but 2n× ↓ (chosen 2026-10-09, over 2 presses per movie with ←/→).
  - OK on Watch plays directly (resume if there is progress); OK on Details opens the movie detail with focus on Play/Resume. Back also goes straight to the section bar from Details (the bookcase silently jumps back to movie 1 of shelf 1, Watch selected). Back from the movie detail lands on the same movie with Details selected. ↑ from Watch on the first shelf goes to the top bar.
  - **Spine color**: from the poster's BlurHash (`ImageBlurHashes`, first 4 characters = average color), same hue at 15% lightness. If the logo itself is dark (average color from the logo's BlurHash, lightness < 38%), a light spine (80% lightness). No extra request, no bitmap analysis on the device.
  - Continue watching shelf: progress as a line in the progress gradient along the left edge of the spine.
- **Library** (chosen 2026-10-10; Paper: "Bibliotheek v2 — Serie, alleen tegels"): the overview of everything, to find something specific. No hero and no backdrop: as many tiles as possible.
  - At the top one row (1× ↓): choice chips **Movie / Series** (last choice remembered), a compact **search field** ("Search for a title"; OK opens the keyboard, the grid filters as you type, by title, ignoring case and accents) and **Sort: A–Z**; each OK the next order: A–Z → Recently added → Year → Rating (community score) → A–Z, remembered. On the right the count ("44 movies", or "3 for “godf”").
  - Below it a **grid of 8 columns** (2× ↓): posters of 92 × 138 dp (same URL as the posters in the rows: from the cache), 18 dp apart, with **the title below** in `bodySmall` (one line, ellipsis; focused: off-white). Exception to "no title under posters": here you search and compare. Two rows in view and the third half, so you see there is more.
  - ↑/↓ keeps the column; ↓ from the controls = tile 1; ↑ from the first row goes to what's above (Movie, Series, search or sort). OK opens the series page or the movie detail; Back lands on the same tile. Back in the grid: straight to the section bar (the grid silently returns to the top).
- **Home section** (formerly Surprise me; revised 2026-10-12; Paper: "Home — stapel, voorstel 3 / 5"): "this is what you'll watch", so you start from Home without having to go to another section. A **stack of at most 5 suggestions**, one large at a time: backdrop over the whole screen, above the logo a **label** saying why it's there (small caps in `accent` with a dot: Continue watching, New episode, Next episode, Recently added, Something for tonight), metadata, 3 lines of description, one button **Play**/**Resume** (focus) and the tech line. Bottom left "‹ previous · n / m · next ›" like Movies in detail view. **←/→ on Play pages through** (no wrap-around; suggestion n = (n−1)× →); the focus stays on Play, the backdrop comes after ~300 ms of rest. Order (`rankHome`), within a group most recently touched first:
  1. half-watched movie or episode, touched in the last **3 days** (Continue watching);
  2. **new episode** of a series you follow: the previous one watched, this one added after that, at most **14 days** old;
  3. continue with what you touched in the last **30 days**: half watched, or the next episode after a watched one (same rule as the Continue watching row in Series);
  4. unwatched movies from the last **30 days**, newest first (Recently added);
  5. fallback: older Continue watching, then one random unwatched movie (Something for tonight).
  A series plays the episode of the suggestion. If there is nothing (all watched, no series in progress), a message in place of the suggestion. The stack stays fixed until something changes (no randomness except the fallback). If you leave the section, the stack silently goes to suggestion 1. Every time the section reappears (from another section, another screen or the player) it refreshes, because something may have been watched elsewhere (2026-10-12); the old one stays until the new one is there, and if that fails, the old one stays. Back from the player also refreshes: the old one stays until the new one is there, then suggestion 1 (if you paged meanwhile, the shown one stays). Navigation: ↑ from Play = section bar; Back = section bar, and the stack silently goes to suggestion 1.
- **Movie detail**: backdrop over the whole screen, logo, metadata, the full description (chosen 2026-10-09; 8-line fallback so the buttons stay in view), one row of buttons: Resume/Play (focus), From the start (only when resuming), Watched (not watched: "Mark as watched"; watched: "✓ Watched"; OK toggles). Below that one tech line in `labelMedium` capitals: per stream what the file has, with an arrow to how it plays here when that differs, and at the end what the server does (Direct play, Remux, Audio converted, Video converted). E.g. "4K · DOLBY VISION · TRUEHD ATMOS → EAC3 ATMOS TRACK · DIRECT PLAY", "4K · DV P7 → HDR10 · AC3 · DIRECT PLAY" or "4K · HDR10 · TRUEHD ATMOS → PCM 7.1 · DIRECT PLAY" (2026-10-10): so you see in advance whether it plays directly, and otherwise whether picture or sound is the cause. AAC etc. get no arrow to PCM (every device decodes that). No rows below.
- **Series page, everything on one page**: no separate season or episode screens.
  - Top block (fixed, doesn't scroll): backdrop, title/logo, one line of metadata, description with a fixed height of 3 lines, button "Continue · S3 · E4" with a fixed width of 300 dp (initial focus; wide enough for French, the time left truncated with "…"). No extra season indicator: the row headings already say where you are.
  - The season list always starts at season 1, directly below the button. "Where was I" is the Continue button, not the scroll position.
  - When the focus rests ~300 ms on an episode, this block shows that episode (title, 3 lines of description, duration).
  - Below that a row per season with the heading "Season 3 · 4 of 24 watched" and episodes as 16:9 cards from left to right.
  - Status per card: watched = check mark and dimmed image (via a `ColorFilter` on the image, not via alpha); half watched = progress bar; new = no marking.
  - Below each episode card one line of text: "5 · Title" in `labelMedium`, with ellipsis. Here text under the card is needed, otherwise episodes can't be told apart.
  - ↓ from "Continue" goes to season 1, episode 1. Back from the player restores focus on the played episode.
  - **Marking as watched** (chosen 2026-10-09): after Continue two buttons. "Mark S3 · E4 as watched" marks the episode of Continue, after which Continue moves on (repeated OK = catch up to where you were elsewhere); when there's nothing left to mark it disappears and the focus goes to its neighbour. "Mark series as watched" / "✓ Series watched" toggles the whole series. At the end of each season row one card (16:9, flat surface) "Mark season as watched" / "✓ Season watched". A single episode can only be reset via the season. Everything is at the end, so the count (season n = n× ↓, episode m = (m−1)× →) stays the same.
  - This is the same pattern as rows on Home (`LazyColumn` with `LazyRow`s); only visible rows are drawn, so 12 seasons cost no more than 2.
