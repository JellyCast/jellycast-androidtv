# Contributing

Ideas and improvements are welcome. Open an issue or a pull request. If it fits "smooth comes before pretty", I'm happy to take a look.

- [CLAUDE.md](CLAUDE.md) and [docs/setup.md](docs/setup.md) have the stack, the tooling and the commands.
- [.claude/rules/](.claude/rules/) has the product principles, design, architecture and TV UX rules.
- [NOTES.md](NOTES.md) has the decisions so far and why they were made.

Keep a pull request to one change, and say in its description what you tested and on which device.

By opening a pull request you agree that your contribution is licensed under the [MIT license](LICENSE) of this repository. No separate agreement is needed.
